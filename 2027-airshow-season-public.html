Worth knowing

  • Only double bill: The Great New England Air Show (Chicopee, MA, May 22–23) is the only 2027 show with both the Blue Angels and the Thunderbirds.
  • May 1–2 conflict: Hill AFB (Layton, UT) and Langley AFB (Hampton, VA) both claim the Thunderbirds the same weekend — but the team's own preliminary schedule lists only Langley; Hill's billing comes from the base/organizer (announced Oct 6, 2026, tickets on sale). Unresolved until the final schedule.
  • USAFA graduation date: the preliminary schedule says May 27, but the Academy's dates point to June 3, 2027 — verify before traveling.
  • Atlantic City: the organizer announced June 4–6, 2027 dates with the lineup unfinalized, conflicting with the May 29–30 Blue Angels billing — in doubt.
  • Stuart: the Nov 12–14 dates and any 2027 headliner could not be verified in public sources.
  • Florida International (Punta Gorda): confirmed no 2027 show — it moves to April 2028.
  • Beale (Apr 17–18): show confirmed, headlined by the U-2 Dragon Lady demo; a Thunderbirds billing there is unconfirmed.
  • Dyess (Apr 24–25): tentative — single directory source; the Dyess 7 FSS page lists Apr 23–24, and no 2027 headliner is confirmed.
  • Air races moved: there is no 2027 Reno airshow; the National Championship Air Races are in Roswell, NM, Sep 15–19.
